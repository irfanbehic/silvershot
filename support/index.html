Common questions

Where are my photos?

In your Photos library, in an album called Silvershot. Every photo is saved there the moment you take it, so deleting or updating the app never removes them.

Which looks are free?

Silver ’06, Flip ’05 and Party Flash, with photo and video, no watermark and no limit.

Why does my photo have a watermark?

It was taken with a Pro look. You can use every look for free; photos from the Pro looks save with a small Silvershot watermark. The app shows this over the viewfinder before every shot. The viewfinder itself never shows the watermark.

I bought Pro. Can I remove the watermark from photos I already took?

Yes. Right after buying, the app offers to save clean copies of your watermarked photos. The watermarked ones stay in your library; the clean copies are added next to them.

I bought Pro on another iPhone

Tap Restore purchase on the paywall or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In Silvershot's Settings, tap Manage subscription, which opens Apple's own screen. You can also cancel in the Settings app on your iPhone, under your name, then Subscriptions. The one-time purchase has nothing to cancel.

The camera screen is black

Check that Silvershot is allowed to use the camera in the Settings app, under Apps, then Silvershot. If another app is using the camera, close it and come back. You can still import photos without the camera.

My photos are not being saved

Silvershot needs access to Photos to save. If it was turned off, your shots wait safely inside the app and are saved as soon as you allow Photos again in the Settings app.

Does Silvershot add where I took a photo?

Only if you turn on Save location in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on.
