What is free

Three looks are free with photo and video, no watermark and no limit. The other looks can be used too; photos and videos taken with them are saved with a Silvershot watermark, and the app says so before every shot.

Silvershot Pro

Silvershot Pro unlocks every look without the watermark. The app shows the full price of each option above the purchase button before you buy:

  • A monthly subscription, renewing every month until cancelled.
  • A yearly subscription, renewing every year until cancelled.
  • A one-time purchase, which does not renew.

Once, after you first close the paywall, the app may offer the yearly plan with a lower price for its first year. The offer shows the first year's price and the price it renews at before you buy.

There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings on your iPhone; the app links to that screen in Settings. Purchases can be restored on the paywall and in Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer.

Silvershot Pro can be shared with your family through Family Sharing for the yearly plan and the one-time purchase.
