Privacy Policy

Silvershot has no accounts and no server for your pictures. Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone and in your own Photos library.

Your photos and videos

Everything you shoot is saved to your Photos library, in an album called Silvershot. Photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. A photo you import is read from your library on your iPhone and is not sent anywhere.

The app also keeps a few files of its own on your iPhone: a shot waiting to be saved if Photos is not available yet, and clean copies of photos taken with a locked look so that you can save them without the watermark after buying Pro. These live only inside the app and are removed when you delete it.

Location

Location is off unless you turn on Save location in Settings. When it is on, the place a photo was taken is written into that photo on your iPhone, the same way the Camera app does it. It is never sent to us.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app people use, Silvershot sends anonymous product events to PostHog, for example that a photo was taken with a certain look, that the paywall was shown, or that a setting was changed. Each event carries an anonymous random ID that is not linked to you. PostHog works out the country and city from the connection and does not keep your IP address.

These events never contain your photos or videos, thumbnails, file names, where or when a photo was taken, photo metadata, your precise location, or anything you type.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Silvershot uses RevenueCat to check whether Silvershot Pro is active, using an anonymous app user ID. Your payment details never reach the app or the developer.

Children

Silvershot is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete any photo from your Photos library as you would any other.
  • Turn Save location off in Settings at any time.
  • Delete the app, which removes everything it kept on your iPhone. Photos already in your library stay there.
